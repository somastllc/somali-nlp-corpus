# Somali NLP Corpus 1.0.0

First release of the cleaned Somali corpus: **7,913,008 documents, 826,973,689 words and
1,129,084,663 tokens** (v2 ByteLevel BPE, 1.3653 tokens per word) from 14 public sources.

The data is published as a gated dataset on Hugging Face:
[`somast/somali-nlp-corpus`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/somast/somali-nlp-corpus).
Corpus files are not attached to this release; they are large and stay on Hugging Face.

## Two license tiers

There is no single license for the corpus. Every record keeps the license of the dataset it
came from, and the release splits records into two configurations:

| Config | Records | Licenses |
|---|---:|---|
| `open` | 6,955,948 | ODC-BY, CC0-1.0, Apache-2.0, CC-BY-SA-4.0, MIT, CC-BY-4.0 |
| `non-commercial` | 957,060 | NCGL-UK-2.0, CC-BY-NC-SA-3.0, CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 |

```python
from datasets import load_dataset
data = load_dataset("somast/somali-nlp-corpus", "open", split="train", revision="v1.0.0")
```

You are responsible for following each record's license. `ATTRIBUTION.md` lists the upstream
credits, the Glot500 sub-source licenses, and the Common Crawl and share-alike notices.

## What changed since the 2026-09-24 build

- Per-record licenses, including Glot500 sub-source licenses. Glot500 rows from `mtdata`,
  `Earthlings`, `Bible_crawled`, `Bible_pbc`, and rows with an unknown sub-source are excluded
  (303,550 rows).
- QuranEnc, Tanzil, and Somali TinyStories removed because their redistribution terms are not
  confirmed.
- Deep-clean fixes: HTML tags no longer join words, malformed tags are removed, the length
  floor is re-applied, stale quality flags are recomputed, and exact duplicates created by
  cleaning are dropped.
- Phone numbers are masked, alongside URLs and emails.
- License-tiered Parquet export with checksums, a dataset card, and an attribution file.

## Known limits

- Language tags are detector guesses. 107,326 records are tagged with a language other than
  Somali, almost all of them NLLB sentences.
- About 28 records keep a 12 to 14 digit phone number after a phone keyword. Names and other
  personal details in news text are not masked.
- Quality was measured automatically; there was no human review of dialect or register.
- Share-alike terms (CC-100, Wikipedia, FLORES-200) have not had a legal review.

## Files

- `SHA256SUMS`: checksums of every Parquet shard.
- `manifest.json`: record counts per tier, source, license, and Glot500 sub-source.
- `ATTRIBUTION.md`: upstream credits and notices.
- `somali-bpe-v2.json`: the 48k ByteLevel BPE tokenizer.
