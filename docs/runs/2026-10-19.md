# Corpus run — 2026-10-19 (14 sources, release 1.0.0)

Measured build of the fourteen-source Track A corpus that ships as release 1.0.0. It follows
the deep-clean fixes, the license policy (per-record licenses, Glot500 sub-source licenses),
the removal of Somali TinyStories, QuranEnc and Tanzil, and phone-number masking. A
sixteen-source build was made earlier the same day; this run replaces it, and the sixteen-source
figures are no longer current. Every figure below comes from the per-run files under `reports/`
(not tracked in Git) or from `tokenizer/*.json`.

**Machine:** Intel Core i7-1185G7, 8 logical CPUs, 31 GiB RAM, Linux. No GPU is used; every
stage is CPU-bound. This is a smaller machine than the 2026-09-24 run (16 cores, 64 GB), so
wall times are not comparable.

## What changed since 2026-09-24

- **License policy.** Each record's `license` is its own upstream dataset's license. Glot500
  rows take the license of their `dataset` column (kept as `provenance.subsource`); rows from
  `mtdata`, `Earthlings`, `Bible_crawled`, `Bible_pbc`, and rows with a missing or unknown
  sub-source, are excluded at merge (303,550 rows). XL-Sum is recorded as CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.
- **Sources removed:** Somali TinyStories (no license), QuranEnc and Tanzil (redistribution
  terms unconfirmed).
- **Phone numbers** are masked in deep clean: international numbers, Somali local mobile
  numbers, and local numbers after a phone keyword.
- **HTML tags** no longer join words: block-level tags (`<br>`, `<p>`, `<div>`, ...) leave a
  line break when stripped. Malformed tags such as `<span;>` and `<br< span="">` are removed.
- **OPUS cleanup** removes only a complete trailing tag instead of cutting everything after
  the last `<`.
- **Length floor re-applied** after deep clean (25 words for documents, 5 for sentences).
- **Stale quality flags** (`html_remnant`, `mostly_numbers`, `high_symbol_ratio`) are
  recomputed from the final text, so repaired records are no longer left on review.
- **Exact duplicates created by deep clean** are dropped in the deep-clean writer, first
  seen wins, across all sources (53,189 records in this run).
- **Paragraph dedup** compares word order, not vocabulary.
- **A lone carriage return** becomes a line break instead of being deleted.
- **Near-dedup memory:** the stage no longer holds document text in memory.

after a phone keyword remain; they are listed under known limits in the dataset card.

Records under 25 words occur only in the sentence sources (`nllb` 3,698,776, `mt560` 29,959,
`opus` 4,901), which use a 5-word floor.

The non-`so` tags are almost all NLLB. They are the language detector's guess for each
sentence, not an annotation, and they are kept as tagging metadata rather than used to reject
sentence records.

Every record's license is one the release export classifies: 6,955,948 records are in the
`open` tier and 957,060 in `non-commercial`.
